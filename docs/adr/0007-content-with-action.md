# A change takes effect in the reply that narrates it

The original held a Field's content back until the reply after its Trigger Action ("action, then content"), so a Description describing the character as they are now could not jump ahead of the change. But the reply narrating a change then never knew what it was changing into: a morph into a form with written Fields, or a transformation into Kael's wolf, was improvised at the moment it mattered most (found replaying a SillyTavern chat on 2026-11-01). Now an Entry or Exit Action's change takes effect in the same reply that is asked to narrate it, the Action itself keeping the order; holding the content back until confirmed remains a per-Field and per-Alter-Ego choice (Content With Action off) for slow reveals.

## Consequences

Turning Content With Action off is the original's behaviour; a definition saved before this change reads as on.
